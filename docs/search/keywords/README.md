# Search > Keywords

The reader who wants exact words writes them like on Google: `"red card"` for a phrase, `referee, VAR`
for one or the other, `referee -rugby` to leave rugby out. As soon as a quote, a comma or a `-word` is
written, the search is no longer by meaning: the words are searched as written, in SQL, on the news of
the last 30 days. When asking for every word at once finds almost nothing, the wider search is counted
and offered, never done on its own.

## Where

- Client: `client/src/pages/Search.jsx` (`SearchPage.handleSubmit`, `handleWiden`, the examples
  `KEYWORD_EXAMPLES` beside the field), `client/src/features/search/components/feed-list/FeedList.jsx`
  ("Show those N"), `client/src/features/search/api/searchApi.js` (`SearchApi.getNews`).
- API: `POST /api/news` (`NewsService.getNews` in `services/news-service.js`), the same route as the
  [search by meaning](../search-by-meaning/README.md).

## How it works

- `Filter.hasOperators` (`services/utils/filter.js`): a straight or curly quote, a comma, or a word
  starting with `-` makes a keyword search. The feeds read are the same as for a sentence, of every language.
- `Filter.parse` turns the text into `{groups, excluded}`: commas outside quotes separate alternatives
  (OR), the terms of an alternative must all be found (AND), `"quoted text"` is exact, `-term` excludes
  (`-"red card"` too).
- `Filter.toPattern` writes one Postgres regex per term: it starts at a word boundary; a word of 3 letters
  or less (`SHORT_WORD_LENGTH`) also ends at one, so "VAR" does not find "Variations"; a short word in
  capitals is an acronym searched case-sensitive (`(?c)`), so "AI" does not find the French "j'ai";
  other words also find their longer forms ("referee" finds "referees"). Each Latin letter is searched
  with and without its accents (`Zürich` finds `Zurich`, `-Barça` leaves out `Barca`).
- `Filter.keywordsSql` joins them: `(t1 AND t2) OR (t3) AND NOT (t4 OR t5)`, each `search_text ~* $n`.
  `FeedModel.searchArticles` runs it on `articles.search_text` (title, description without HTML and RSS
  categories, separated by " | " so a phrase cannot run from one field into the next), generated by
  Postgres and indexed with pg_trgm (`i_articles_search_text`, GIN `gin_trgm_ops`, `db/add_articles_search.sql`),
  with the feeds and the dates, newest first.
- Under 5 news (`MIN_RESULTS`) and when an alternative has several words (`canWiden`), `widen` asks for
  any of the words, the `-words` kept. Its count is answered as `wider: {found, terms, keywords}`, not its
  news: `keywords` is that search written back (`Filter.toKeywords`: `"red card", referee, -rugby`).
  "Show those N" puts `keywords` back in the field and searches again: the search counted, its exact phrases
  and `-words` with it (until 17.10.2026 the terms joined with commas, which lost them; a search kept from
  before has its terms only).
- Nothing found answers `{totalResults: 0, news: [], wider, mode: 'words'}` at once.
- The cards: once per link, then one card per story of the background work; the news in no story are
  grouped by the trigrams of their titles, accents removed (`FeedModel.similarArticlePairs`, `unaccent`),
  joining a group they resemble on average at 0.25, 0.45 for a title under 40 characters
  (`averageLink` in `services/utils/grouping.js`). The same titles at 0.85 are one wording
  (`unionFind`): each card says how many media and how many own headlines, never more than the media.
  Each card carries `hedged`, the words the article uses to say it has no confirmation (`hedgedBy`).
- The facts of an affair go on one card (`withThreads`, 12 facts not found at most); a fact holding an
  excluded word is left out there too (`Filter.matcher`). The answer: `{totalResults, news, wider, mode: 'words'}`.

## Measured

From the code comments and [the former README](../../archive/readme-features-2026-10-24.md):

- Accents (`bench/accent-patterns.mjs`): 8 to 100 ms a term with the trigram index still used; "Zürich"
  found 52 news of 30 days, 218 with "Zurich". With Latin Extended-A too, up to 330 ms; every Latin
  letter, 1 s.
- Trigram threshold, on forty groups judged by hand: 0.30 recovers 16 of them, 0.25 recovers 39, neither
  puts two media on a news they do not share. Single links instead of the average left a group of 27
  articles, the average never more than 9. The short-title guard drops 32 pairs of 182 on a wide search.
- One accent ("Higuaín" against "Higuain") moves a pair from 0.325 to 0.294: hence `unaccent`.
- Stories against trigrams (`bench/story-cards.mjs`, eleven searches of a week): the stories were right 74
  times, the trigrams 31.
